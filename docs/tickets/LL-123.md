# LL-123 — Read a statement's summary figures by their labels, and check the statement's own math

**Branch** `feat/ll-123-statement-figures` · **Status** implemented · **Origin** owner report (2026-09-28): statement_05312025.pdf printed Previous Balance 3,814.15 · Total Deposits 9,800.00 · Total Checks and Debits 9,554.08 · Balance This Statement 4,087.07, and the app showed total credits 3,814.15, total debits 4,087.07, ending 9,554.08 — "Way off"; "start LL-123" on the proposed fix · **Decision** ADR-034 amendment · **Migration** 0051

The AI now copies every line of the statement's account summary with its printed label, and the app decides each line's
role from the label. A swap like the owner's can no longer happen: "Previous Balance" is always the beginning balance.
Every figure must appear on the statement. Money in and money out add up each category, such as deposits plus other
credits. The first check is now the statement's own math: beginning + money in − money out = ending. If it fails, the
AI re-reads the statement and is told the gap. The review shows what was read, the statement's math and the lines'
math, and lets the reviewer correct a misread figure.
