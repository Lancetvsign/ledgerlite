## §11 — End-of-ticket report

any other member company, take the untaken lines as that company's expenses (both sides posted INTERCOMPANY in

**Test results:** lint ✓ / typecheck ✓ / unit ✓ / build ✓ (`npm run ci`); e2e ✓ locally (bank-import,
organizations, reconciliation); integration + migration on CI.
**Accounting implications:** every card line still hits the cardholder's card (the card reconciles); PERSONAL
never touches the P&L; a taken line is the taker's expense with an intercompany payable, mirrored to the cent
by the cardholder's receivable; corrections by reversal (give back) only.
**Security implications:** visible ⇔ assignable; posting rights needed in both companies; no organization-level
role; the viewer's company comes from the session, the cardholder from the batch.
**Decisions made not specified in the ticket:** assign from the cardholder's page dropped (the owner's flow is
"open the other company and take"); Personal account chosen per line (default Owner Distributions) instead of a
settings column; only card statements shareable; un-sharing allowed anytime, takers keep their lines; PERSONAL
excluded from history suggestions; one transaction per taken line (like `post`), not one per submit.
**Known limitations:** no bulk "take all"; the register in the taker links its INTERCOMPANY entry to the journal
page (the batch belongs to the cardholder).
**Recommended follow-up:** LL-098 intercompany report + GL-T029; LL-099 intercompany bank transfers + settlement.
