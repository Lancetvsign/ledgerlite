## §11 — End-of-ticket report

**Ticket:** LL-099 — mark a bank line as a transfer with another member company; from the other company,
match it from its own statement by amount and timing; both banks reconcile; repayment zeroes the Due pair.
**Files changed:** `src/server/bank-import/intercompany.ts` (new: `transferCounterparts`,
`findIntercompanyCandidates`, `markIntercompanyTransfer`, `matchIntercompanyTransfer`, pair choice),
`index.ts` (decisions `intercompany_transfer` / `match_intercompany`, `intercompanyCandidate` on the view,
`intercompany` in the result), `errors.ts`; `src/validation/bank-import.ts`; review page + `line-action`,
`line-counterpart` (new), `review-state`, `actions.ts`; docs.
**Migrations created:** none — everything rides on LL-097's `intercompany_group_id` and LL-096's pairs.
**DB constraints added:** none (the group unique is mapped to `TRANSFER_ALREADY_MATCHED`).
**Tests added:** `tests/integration/bank-import-intercompany.test.ts` — mark → candidate → match (same group,
entries exact, mirror, both banks reconcile with `fromImport`, candidate gone once matched); settlement of
LL-097 card charges through the existing pair (no second pair, both Due accounts to zero); both sides marked
independently (two groups, still mirrored) and the payer-holds-receivable creation rule; counterpart
validation incl. an ADMIN-only outsider and self; match validation (amount, direction, window, stale id);
two simultaneous matches of one group → exactly one, the other `TRANSFER_ALREADY_MATCHED`; double submit
no-op; closed period blocks with nothing posted. E2E: mark in the payer, match from the payee's card
statement, the intercompany report mirrored on both sides.
**Test results:** lint ✓ / typecheck ✓ / unit ✓ / build ✓ (`npm run ci`); bank-import + reports e2e ✓
locally; integration on CI.
**Accounting implications:** each side posts against its own statement account; the mirror holds by
construction; settlement is the ordinary transfer flow on the existing pair.
**Security implications:** counterparts and candidates are limited to member companies the actor holds a
`journal.post` role in (visible ⇔ actionable); the match re-proves the other side inside the transaction.
**Decisions made not specified in the ticket:** one relationship per company pair with signed balances;
payer-holds-receivable on creation; no "Link" step; the marking side needs no permission in the counterpart
beyond the role-based visibility rule.
**Known limitations:** the candidate is one per line (nearest date); a company that lacks any member with
posting rights in both companies cannot mark or match.
**Recommended follow-up:** Gate 7 (human review of the intercompany model across two real companies).
