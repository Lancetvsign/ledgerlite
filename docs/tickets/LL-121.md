# LL-121 — No reversal is ever dated before the entry it reverses

**Branch** `feat/ll-121-reversal-not-before-original` · **Status** implemented · **Origin** owner's choice for LL-121 (2026-09-28), the ledger gap LL-119's review recorded · **Decision** ADR-044 amendment · **Migration** 0050

The ledger now refuses a reversal dated before the entry it reverses, in the service and in the database. A date
someone chose that is too early is refused with a clear message. A date the app filled in (today) is moved to the
original's own date when the original is later. So voiding a future-dated invoice or bill, reversing a future-dated
journal entry, un-marking an intercompany transfer or giving back a shared card line lands on the original's date,
where before it would have dated the reversal before the thing it reverses.
