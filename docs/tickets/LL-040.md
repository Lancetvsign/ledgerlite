# LL-040 — Customers


**Branch** `feat/ll-040-customers`

> Implement LL-040: the customer entity — the party an invoice bills. First ticket of
> Sprint 4 (Accounts Receivable). Customers are a prerequisite for invoices (LL-041+),
> which reference them.
>
> ### Entity
> A `customers` table, company-scoped, with `name` (required), optional
> `customer_number` (unique per company when present), `email`, `phone`,
> `billing_address`, `notes`, and a `status` (`ACTIVE`/`INACTIVE`). **No balance
> column** — a customer's open balance is derived from posted journal lines against
> Accounts Receivable (invariant 2), never stored.
>
> ### Tenancy is structural
> Carry the standing `UNIQUE (company_id, id)`, and wire `journal_lines
> (company_id, customer_id)` to it with a composite FK so a journal line can never
> reference another company's customer — exactly as accounts are wired. `customer_id`
> is nullable (most lines have no customer); the FK is skipped when it is NULL.
>
> ### Service & rules
> `createCustomer` / `updateCustomer` / `deactivateCustomer` / `listCustomers`, each
> company-scoped and authorization-gated (new `customer.view` / `customer.manage`
> capabilities). No hard delete (ADR-006): deactivate only, and an inactive customer
> stays queryable for the invoices that reference it. Every mutation records an audit
> event inside its own transaction (new `CUSTOMER_*` audit actions).
>
> ### Tests
> Create/update/deactivate (audited); duplicate customer number rejected; NULL numbers
> distinct; company-scoped listing; cross-company update → `CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND`;
> authorization (READ_ONLY may view, not manage; non-member denied); and the
> **structural** proof — a raw-SQL journal line referencing another company's customer
> is rejected by the composite FK, with the service bypassed. Register `customers` in
> the tenant-isolation harness (the completeness test fails CI otherwise).
