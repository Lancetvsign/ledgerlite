# LL-055 — Reporting UI: Trial Balance, A/R Aging, Customer Statement


**Branch** `feat/ll-055-reporting-ui`

**Status** Backlog · **Origin** Sprint 5 plan · LL-046 recommended-next. See
[SPRINT-5-PLAN.md](../SPRINT-5-PLAN.md). **UI last in the sprint** — depends on LL-054
(statements) and, for the aging's credit column, LL-051.

> Surface the reports that exist today only as services. Three read-only screens under
> `/reports`, `report.view`-gated, mirroring the `/accounts` + `/journal` UI patterns. **No
> new schema, no new service** — pure presentation over `getTrialBalance`, `getArAging`, and
> `getCustomerStatement`.
>
> ### Screens
> - **Trial Balance** (`/reports/trial-balance`) — accounts with derived debit/credit
>   balances as of a date; the balanced check shown explicitly.
> - **A/R Aging** (`/reports/aging`) — the bucketed subsidiary (Current / 1–30 / 31–60 /
>   61–90 / 90+) per customer with grand totals; an "as of" date control.
> - **Customer Statement** (`/reports/statement`) — pick a customer + date; open items and
>   activity with opening/closing balance.
>
> ### Rules
> RSC pages that call the services directly; `report.view` enforced server-side (a viewer
> without it sees the same not-found/denied shape as elsewhere). Money is **display-only**,
> formatted from the service's `string` values — **no JavaScript number touches money**
> (ADR-004), no client-side recomputation of totals. An "as of" date is a query param,
> validated server-side (`isCalendarDate`). A nav entry (**Reports**) on the account page.
> `data-testid` throughout for e2e.
>
> ### Tests
> Playwright e2e (production build, per-feature storageState): each report renders with
> correct derived numbers for a seeded company; the aging grand total on screen equals the
> A/R control; a member **without** `report.view` cannot reach the pages; an invalid `asOf`
> is rejected. A lint/grep check that no money value is parsed to a JS number in the report
> components.
>
> ### Not in scope
> A dashboard/landing that composes multiple reports; CSV/PDF export; charts; scheduled or
> emailed reports — all Sprint 6+.

**Accounting implications** None new — pure presentation. Money is printed verbatim from the
services' `NUMERIC(19,4)` strings; no total is recomputed client-side (the trial-balance footer uses
the service's totals, the aging buckets/totals and the statement running balance are the services').

**Security implications** Every screen re-authorizes server-side (`report.view`, enforced by the
services), company comes from the session context (never a URL/query param), and a cross-company /
unknown `customerId` on the statement returns `null` → the "not found" shape (no existence leak, §6).

**Decisions made that were not specified in the ticket** The spec's Customer Statement line
(*"open items and activity"*) is superseded by **ADR-022** (LL-054): the statement screen shows the
period **opening / activity / closing**, and the per-invoice open-items view is the **Aging** screen.
The statement therefore takes a **date range** (from/to), and the Trial Balance shows each account's
net **Balance** alongside the gross Debit/Credit sums (so the footer totals remain the service's,
un-recomputed) — the net column is what reconciles to the aging.

**Known limitations** No dashboard, CSV/PDF export, charts, or scheduled/emailed reports (Sprint 6+).
No open-items itemization on the statement (ADR-022). Single currency.

**Recommended follow-up** Export (CSV/PDF) and a composed dashboard in a later sprint; a
`getCompany`-by-id read would let `requireReportContext` fetch the timezone without listing all
companies (minor).
