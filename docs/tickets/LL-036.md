# LL-036 — General Ledger Regression Suite


**Branch** `test/ll-036-gl-regression-suite`

> Implement LL-036: consolidate the accounting invariant tests into one release-blocking
> suite. Coverage should mostly exist already from LL-030 through LL-035 — this ticket
> organizes it, fills gaps, and makes it a required check.
>
> | ID | Invariant |
> |---|---|
> | GL-T001 | Balanced entry posts successfully |
> | GL-T002 | Unbalanced entry is rejected |
> | GL-T003 | Cross-company account reference is rejected |
> | GL-T004 | Posting into a closed period is rejected |
> | GL-T005 | Duplicate idempotency key does not duplicate a posting |
> | GL-T006 | Reversal exactly negates the original |
> | GL-T007 | Editing a posted entry is rejected |
> | GL-T008 | Zero-line and single-line postings are rejected |
> | GL-T009 | Inactive account posting is rejected |
> | GL-T010 | Tenant boundary fails safely and leaks no existence |
> | GL-T011 | Decimal precision remains exact at four places |
> | GL-T012 | A failed transaction leaves no partial records |
> | GL-T013 | Journal source linkage is preserved through reversal |
> | GL-T014 | Concurrent identical postings produce exactly one entry |
> | GL-T015 | Trial balance derives correctly and balances |
>
> Each case asserts the **specific error code**, not merely that something threw.
> Each ends by running all four LL-034 invariant helpers.
>
> **Then run an adversarial pass.** Spawn a fresh subagent with no knowledge of the
> implementation:
>
> > *"You have authorized access to LedgerService in a LedgerLite test environment.
> > Your goal is to leave the database in a state where any of these is false: every
> > posted entry balances; no posted entry references another company's account; no
> > source transaction posted twice; no posted entry was modified after posting; no
> > partial journal exists. Use concurrency, malformed input, boundary values, unusual
> > decimal precision, date boundaries, period transitions during posting, reversal
> > edge cases, and any ordering of operations you can construct. Report every state
> > you reach, including ones you are unsure about."*
>
> Every finding becomes a permanent regression test. Register the suite as a required
> GitHub check with no ability to merge on failure.
