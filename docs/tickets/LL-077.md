# LL-077 — Apply bank-import lines to open invoices / bills

**Branch** `feat/ll-077-bank-import-apply` · **Status** implemented (see §11) · **Origin** follow-up to
LL-076 (ADR-034 named reconciliation-to-documents as the next step). Design: **ADR-035**.

## Problem

A customer's deposit on a bank statement, categorised to *Sales Revenue*, double-counts revenue already
recognised on the invoice and leaves the invoice OPEN (A/R overstated). Mirror for a vendor payment vs.
an open bill. The reviewer needs to **settle the document**, and A/R / A/P may only move through their
documents (ADR-016/018/023).

## Decisions (product owner: "defaults")

Atomic with the line via extracted payment cores · whole line → ONE document (partial payment of the
document OK, over-application rejected) · amount-match suggestion preselects the document.

**Accounting implications:** applied lines are ordinary customer payments / bill payments — A/R and
A/P move only through their documents, so the open-balance derivations, the aging⇔control release gates
(GL-T018/T025) and the void guards cover them with no change. No new reduction source. `BANK_IMPORT`
entries still never touch A/R / A/P. Revenue and expense are recognised once, by the document.

**Security implications:** `journal.post` still gates the flow; apply decisions additionally require
`payment.create` / `bill_payment.create`. Open documents come from company-scoped lists, so a foreign or
closed document reads as "not open" (no existence leak). Post-once is structural: the line is locked and
re-checked inside the same transaction that creates the payment.

**Decisions made not specified in the ticket:** the cores are exported (not a separate module) and
documented as MECHANICAL, mirroring `postEntryCore`; the category path also takes the line lock (a
uniform post-once mechanism); payment `reference` = statement description (≤100), `memo` = line number,
`method` = "Bank import"; the CHECK constraint (not in the original ask) makes the one-target rule
structural; `applied` is reported as a subset of `posted`.

**Known limitations:** one document per line (splitting stays on the payment forms); no unapplied
credit for an overpayment (a line larger than the document is refused); matching is by exact amount
only; the open-document selects are company-wide (can be long).

**Recommended follow-up:** LL-078 bank reconciliation (statement balance vs. ledger cash, cleared
lines); split applications; match by reference; unapplied customer credit.
