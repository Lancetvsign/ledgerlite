# LL-054 — Customer statements (reporting service)


**Branch** `feat/ll-054-customer-statements`

**Status** Backlog · **Origin** Sprint 5 plan · LL-046 recommended-next. See
[SPRINT-5-PLAN.md](../SPRINT-5-PLAN.md).

> A per-customer **statement**: the activity and open items behind that customer's A/R
> balance. A pure reporting service like `getTrialBalance` / `getArAging` — **no schema, no
> stored balance** (invariant 2), decimal.js money, `report.view`-gated. Services first; the
> screen is LL-055.
>
> ### Service
> `getCustomerStatement(actorUserId, companyId, customerId, asOf)` returning, for one
> customer as of a date:
> - **Open items** — each OPEN invoice with its open balance (reusing the LL-045 / LL-046
>   open-balance derivation: `total − Σ non-void applications`, and, once LL-051 lands,
>   less applied credits).
> - **Activity** — invoices, payments, credit memos, and refunds over a period, in date
>   order, each with its effect on the running A/R balance.
> - **Totals** — opening balance, period movement, closing balance — where the **closing
>   balance equals that customer's contribution to the A/R control** (the same
>   subsidiary⇔control discipline as the aging).
>
> ### Rules
> `requirePermission('report.view')`, company-scoped, `getDb()` HTTP read client, money as
> `string` summed with decimal.js. A cross-company `customerId` reads as a genuine miss (no
> existence leak). Void invoices/payments/credits are excluded from balances (shown as
> voided in activity if listed).
>
> ### Tests
> The statement's closing balance reconciles to that customer's A/R (sum over the company's
> statements equals the A/R control); company-scoped (another tenant's customer → not found);
> open items match the aging for that customer; a voided payment restores the open item and
> the statement still reconciles; decimal exactness.
>
> ### Not in scope
> The UI (LL-055); PDF/email delivery; multi-currency.

## §11 — End-of-ticket report

**Tests added**
- `customer-statement.test.ts`: opening + running balance across all four A/R document types

- **GL-T021** (release gate): each customer's statement closing balance is their slice of the GL A/R

- integration: customer-statement 8/8 ✅; GL suite 21/21 ✅ (incl. GL-T021). Full integration suite

**Accounting implications** A customer statement is now derivable for any period, exactly, from the
customer-tagged A/R ledger — no stored balance (invariant 2). Its closing balance reconciles to that
customer's contribution to the A/R control (GL-T021), extending the subsidiary⇔control discipline
(ADR-016) to the per-customer level.

**Security implications** `report.view`-gated and company-scoped like the other reports; a
cross-company or unknown `customerId` returns `null`, identical to a genuine miss (no existence leak,
§6). Read-only; no new write path or capability.

**Decisions made that were not specified in the ticket** The ticket sketched a single `asOf`
parameter and an "open items" (current OPEN invoices) section. The approved plan (and ADR-022)
instead ships a **period statement** — signature `(fromDate, toDate)`, since an opening balance
requires a period start — and **omits the open-items section**: the current open-invoice view is the
aging's job (LL-046, surfaced by LL-055), and a historical open-items-as-of-a-past-date needs the
point-in-time aging ADR-016 deferred. This divergence from the ticket text was explicitly approved.

**Known limitations** No open-items itemization on the statement (see above). No PDF/email delivery
and no UI (LL-055). Single currency.

**Recommended follow-up** LL-055 (reporting UI) will surface this alongside the aging. If customers
want an itemized open-items section on the statement, add it from the shared open-balance derivation
(`invoiceReductionsExpr`); a historical open-items-as-of-date view remains gated on point-in-time
aging (ADR-016).
