# LL-106 — Card payments find their paying company on the organization's statements

**Branch** `feat/ll-106-organization-matches` (on LL-105) · **Status** implemented · **Origin** owner request during the Gate 7 manual acceptance (2026-09-24): "In the credit card statement I need to be able to mark a payment as assign to another company without picking the company. It would be better to actively review all of the companies' transactions for a match in real time so I don't have to guess or leave the page to search." · **Decision** ADR-043 amendment · **Migration** none (builds on LL-105's drafts)

A positive card line (a payment or refund) — or any bank-statement line — now shows the other members'
bank-statement lines that mirror it (opposite amount, three-day window, on a bank account, in a company the
reviewer may post in), whatever their state there: **staged** (choose it: this side posts now, their reviewer
sees the match), **posted as an intercompany mark** (today's match candidate), or **decided** some other way
(a warning: undo it there first). The action reads "Paid by another company…" on a card; a found match is
preselected; the company picker remains as a last resort ("Beta Co — no statement line found"); with nothing to
match yet the line **waits** — a draft the submit skips — and while anything waits the page re-reads the server
every 30 s (and on "Check again"), so the match appears the moment the other statement is uploaded.

**Test results:** lint ✓ / typecheck ✓ / unit ✓ / build ✓ (`npm run ci`); bank-import e2e locally; integration on CI.
**Accounting implications:** none new — the posting is LL-099's mark/match; only how the counterpart is named changed
(from a picked company to a statement line the database re-proves).
**Security implications:** candidates come only from companies the actor may post in (`transferCounterparts`); a
statement line named by the browser is re-proven (member, STAGED, bank account, opposite amount, window) and the
company derived from it — never trusted; the company picker is still validated as before.
**Decisions made that were not specified in the ticket:** bank-statement lines get the same matches (a transfer to
another company's bank is the same problem); only a card payment defaults to waiting — a bank transfer with no
match keeps the company picker's default; the chosen statement line is not stored in the draft (the counterpart
company is), so on reload the match from that company is reselected.
**Known limitations:** "real time" is a 30-second re-read plus a button, not a push; a decided line in the other
company is reported, not undone from here; two identical payments on one day are both offered the same match.
**Recommended follow-up:** none required.
