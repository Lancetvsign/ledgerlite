# LL-042 — Invoice finalize, post to the ledger, void

**Branch** `feat/ll-042-invoice-posting`

> Finalize a DRAFT invoice (DRAFT→OPEN): assign its number, freeze it, and post the balanced
> entry to the general ledger through `LedgerService` — Dr Accounts Receivable / Cr Revenue /
> Cr Sales Tax Payable — source-once and atomic. Void an OPEN invoice by reversing that entry.
> Sprint 4 (Accounts Receivable). See **ADR-014**.

## What was built

### Posting (finalize)
`finalizeInvoice` transitions a DRAFT invoice to OPEN in ONE transaction: it locks the invoice
FOR UPDATE, re-derives the posting from the frozen lines (`computeInvoicePosting`, decimal.js),
asserts it equals the stored totals (ADR-013 tripwire), allocates the invoice number, and posts
**Dr A/R = total** (customer-tagged), **Cr revenue by account**, **Cr Sales Tax Payable = tax**.
The entry is `source_type='INVOICE'`, `source_id=invoiceId`; the existing
`journal_entries_source_posted_once` index makes a second posting impossible.

### Void
`voidInvoice` reverses an OPEN invoice's posted entry (`reverseEntryCore`) and marks it VOID,
atomically. The original entry is never edited (invariant 3); the reversal nets every account
to zero (ADR-010).

### Ledger core extraction
`postEntryCore` / `reverseEntryCore` were extracted from the manual posting/reversal paths so a
document service can post/reverse WITHIN its own transaction (atomicity) and authorize at its own
capability. The manual `postJournalEntry` / `reverseJournalEntry` are unchanged externally.

### Accounts + numbering
System-account resolution is structural: a new partial unique index on
`accounts (company_id, system_account_type)`; Sales Tax Payable is flagged `SALES_TAX_PAYABLE`
in the standard chart. Invoice numbers come from `company_counters.next_invoice_number` (gaps
allowed). `updateInvoice` now locks FOR UPDATE so an edit can't clobber a concurrent finalize.

**Accounting implications:** invoices now hit the GL through the one approved mechanism; every
amount is derived and reversible; no account balance is stored (invariant 2 intact). Gapless
numbering was NOT chosen (gaps allowed) — revisit if a jurisdiction requires it.

**Security implications:** finalize/void authorize at `invoice.post` and fail closed; the ledger
cores are mechanical and only reachable through a capability-checked service; cross-company ids
read as a genuine miss (no existence leak); tenancy is structural (composite FKs + the new
system-account index).

**Decisions not specified in the ticket:** ADR-014 (accounts used, atomicity via extracted core,
document-level authz, gaps-allowed numbering, source-once idempotency). Locked `updateInvoice`
FOR UPDATE (a race the OPEN state newly makes reachable).

**Known limitations:** void handles OPEN only (not PAID — payments are LL-043); one Sales Tax
Payable account per company (no per-line tax authority); invoice number is a plain sequence.

**Recommended follow-up:** LL-043 payments (A/R application, and void of a PAID invoice); a
possible `invoice.void` capability if void should be separable from post.
