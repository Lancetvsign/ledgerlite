# LL-041 — Invoice entity


**Branch** `feat/ll-041-invoice-entity`

> Implement LL-041: the invoice entity — `invoices` + `invoice_lines`, and the DRAFT
> service that creates and edits them. Sprint 4 (Accounts Receivable). Posting to the
> ledger, finalize, and void come in LL-042.
>
> ### Schema
> `invoices` (company-scoped): `customer_id` (composite FK → customers), nullable
> `invoice_number` (unique per company, assigned at finalize), `status`
> (`DRAFT`/`OPEN`/`PAID`/`VOID`), `invoice_date`, `due_date`, `memo`, `created_by`, and
> **stored** `subtotal`/`tax_total`/`total`. `invoice_lines`: `invoice_id` (composite FK),
> `line_number`, `description`, `quantity`, `unit_price`, `account_id` (composite FK → the
> revenue account credited), `tax_rate` (%). Both carry the standing `UNIQUE (company_id,
> id)`; every reference is a composite FK, so a cross-tenant customer or account is
> structurally impossible.
>
> ### Totals — ADR-013
> `subtotal`/`tax_total`/`total` are stored but **always** recomputed from the lines by the
> service (`computeInvoiceTotals`, decimal.js, ROUND_HALF_EVEN) and written on every
> create/update — no input carries a total. This is a document total, not an account
> balance (invariant 2 untouched); a regression test asserts stored == recompute(lines).
>
> ### Service
> `createInvoice` / `updateInvoice` (DRAFT only) / `getInvoice` / `listInvoices`, each
> company-scoped and gated on the existing `invoice.view` / `invoice.create` capabilities.
> The customer and every line account are re-validated to be in this company (typed
> `CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND` / `ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND`). Every mutation records an audit event (new
> `INVOICE_CREATED` / `INVOICE_UPDATED` actions).
>
> ### Tests
> `computeInvoiceTotals` exactness (incl. 0.1+0.2); create stores derived totals + audit;
> stored == recompute (ADR-013); get/list scoping; DRAFT-only edit (`INVOICE_NOT_DRAFT`);
> reference validation; authorization (READ_ONLY views but cannot create); and the
> **structural** proof — raw invoice/line referencing another company's customer/account
> rejected by the composite FKs. Register `invoices` and `invoice_lines` in the
> tenant-isolation harness.
