# LL-118 — Say on the review when the AI's re-check was tried and failed

**Branch** `feat/ll-118-reanalysis-failed` · **Status** implemented · **Origin** owner's choice for LL-118 (2026-09-28), the known limitation LL-114 recorded · **Decision** ADR-034 amendment · **Migration** 0049

When a statement's first reading does not match its printed totals, LedgerLite asks the AI to re-check it (LL-109).
If that re-check fails, the first reading is kept (LL-114). The review now says so, and why: for example "the AI
service was busy". It also suggests uploading again later may give a reading that adds up. Before, the reviewer saw
a plain mismatch and could not tell a re-check had been missed.
