# LL-064 — A/P aging + vendor statement

**Branch** `feat/ll-064-ap-aging-statement`

**Status** Backlog · **Origin** Sprint 6 plan (Accounts Payable). See
[SPRINT-6-PLAN.md](../SPRINT-6-PLAN.md). The structural mirror of LL-046 (A/R aging) and LL-054
(customer statement) — pure reporting services, **no schema, no stored balance** (invariant 2).

> The A/P subsidiary and the per-vendor statement, derived entirely from bills, their non-void
> reductions, and the vendor-tagged A/P journal lines.
>
> ### Services
> - **`getApAging(asOf)`** — each OPEN bill's open balance (`total − Σ non-void bill-payments +
>   vendor-credits`, the shared A/P derivation) bucketed by age per vendor, with grand totals. Its
>   grand total **equals the derived A/P control** balance — the subsidiary⇔control tie, the A/P
>   analogue of GL-T018.
> - **`getVendorStatement(vendorId, fromDate, toDate)`** — opening / dated activity with a running
>   balance / closing, from the vendor-tagged A/P lines (mirror ADR-022 / LL-054); closing = that
>   vendor's A/P control slice.
>
> ### Rules
> `report.view`-gated; `getDb()` HTTP read client; money as `string` summed with decimal.js; a
> cross-company / unknown `vendorId` returns `null` (no leak, §6).
>
> ### Tests
> Bucketing; a partial payment / vendor credit reduces the bucket; **GL-T023** — the aging grand
> total == the A/P control, and Σ vendor-statement closings == the control (the per-vendor
> decomposition), across a bill → payment → credit → void lifecycle; date validation; decimal
> exactness.
>
> ### Not in scope
> The UI (LL-065); point-in-time historical aging (deferred, ADR-016); multi-currency.

**Accounting implications** The A/P subsidiary now reconciles to the GL A/P control both in
aggregate (aging grand total) and per vendor (statement closing), derived entirely from bills, their
non-void reductions, and the vendor-tagged A/P lines — no stored balance. A/P being credit-natural,
a vendor's balance is `Σ(credit − debit)`; every A/P line is vendor-tagged and manual A/P posting is
forbidden (ADR-023), so the per-vendor decomposition sums to the whole control.

**Security implications** Both services are `report.view`-gated and company-scoped; a cross-company /
unknown `vendorId` returns `null` (no existence leak, §6); `asOf` and date ranges are validated
calendar dates.

**Decisions made that were not specified in the ticket** (1) Extracted the shared aging-bucket
helpers into `src/server/reports/aging.ts` and refactored `ar-aging.ts` to use them, rather than
duplicating (single-source discipline; A/R aging re-verified). (2) Used **GL-T026** for the new
release-gate test — the spec said "GL-T023", but GL-T023–T025 already exist. (3) Vendor-statement
columns are `charge` (Cr A/P) / `payment` (Dr A/P), the A/P-natural naming, vs the customer
statement's `charge`/`credit`. ADR-024 records the reconciliation decision.

**Known limitations** Current open balances only — historical point-in-time aging remains deferred
(ADR-016). No UI yet (LL-065). No credit-balance vendors (a vendor net-negative A/P is out of scope,
ADR-023).

**Recommended follow-up** LL-065 (A/P reporting UI — surfaces `getApAging` / `getVendorStatement`),
then GATE 5 (closes Sprint 6).
