# LL-127 — Statement upload: faster AI read, and never lose an upload to the time limit

**Branch** `fix/ll-127-extraction-time-budget` · **Status** implemented · **Origin** the owner's report (2026-09-30): every upload of statement_05312025.pdf ends on "This page couldn't load" · **Decision** ADR-049 · **Migration** 0052 (widens a CHECK)

The AI read took over four minutes at its default effort, and when the re-check then began the 300 s platform limit killed the request. Now:
- the read runs at low effort (Sonnet 5 unchanged) — far shorter;
- the model calls share a 240 s budget: a first read that overruns fails with a clear notice, and a re-check that has no time (or is cut off) leaves the first read staged with a note on the review.
