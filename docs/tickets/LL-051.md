# LL-051 — Customer credits & refunds


**Branch** `feat/ll-051-customer-credits-refunds`

**Status** Backlog · **Origin** Sprint 5 plan · see [SPRINT-5-PLAN.md](../SPRINT-5-PLAN.md).
Depends on LL-050 (control-account protection) landing first.

> Give A/R the two movements it is missing: a **credit memo** (reduce what a customer owes,
> no cash) and a **cash refund** (return money on a credit or overpayment). Both post through
> `LedgerService`, customer-tagged, so the A/R control moves correctly — and the A/R
> subsidiary (aging / statements) must be extended to stay reconciled, because unapplied
> credit makes a customer's true balance *less* than "sum of open invoices" (ADR-016 flagged
> exactly this). **This is the ticket's real design work; it needs an ADR.**
>
> ### Accounting
> - **Credit memo** — `Dr Sales Returns & Allowances` (a contra-revenue account) `/ Cr
>   Accounts Receivable` (customer-tagged). Creates customer credit that can be **applied**
>   to one or more open invoices (reusing the LL-043 application mechanism) or left unapplied.
> - **Cash refund** — return cash against an unapplied credit / overpayment: `Dr Accounts
>   Receivable` (customer-tagged, clearing the credit) `/ Cr` the deposit/cash account.
>   `CUSTOMER_REFUND` was reserved as a source type back in LL-043.
>
> ### The ADR decision
> Once A/R can carry credit, `Σ open-invoice balances ≠ A/R control`. Decide and record:
> whether the **aging and statements gain a "credits / unapplied" component** and how the
> subsidiary reconciles to control with credits present. **GL-T018 must be extended** so the
> release gate proves the tie *including* credit memos and refunds — the invariant is the
> point of the feature, not an afterthought.
>
> ### Entity & tenancy
> A `credit_memos` (and applications) shape mirroring invoices/payments: company-scoped, the
> standing `UNIQUE (company_id, id)`, composite FKs `(company_id, customer_id)` and
> `(company_id, account_id)` so nothing crosses a tenant, positivity checks, `status`
> (`OPEN`/`APPLIED`/`VOID`), and **no stored balance** (a customer's credit is derived from
> lines, invariant 2). One migration.
>
> ### Service & rules
> `createCreditMemo` / `applyCreditMemo` / `refundCustomer` / `voidCreditMemo`, each
> company-scoped, authorization-gated (reuse/extend the A/R capabilities), posting atomically
> via `getDbTx()` + the ledger core, audited inside the tx. Void is by **reversal**. A refund
> deposit/cash account must be a real asset (mirror the LL-043 `DEPOSIT_ACCOUNT_INVALID`
> guard); credit-memo and refund line accounts obey the LL-050 control-account rule.
>
> ### Tests
> Credit memo reduces the customer's open balance and the A/R control by the same amount;
> applied credit clears an invoice; a refund moves cash and clears the credit; void reverses
> to zero; company-scoped; **the extended reconciliation (GL-T018) holds** across invoices +
> payments + credits + refunds, including after a void.
>
> ### Not in scope
> Vendor credits (that is Accounts Payable, Sprint 6+); multi-currency.

## §11 report

**Ticket:** LL-051 — customer credit memos (applied to invoices).
**Branch:** `feat/ll-051-credit-memos`

**Scope note:** built **Model A** (credit memos applied to a specific invoice), decided with the
product owner. **Cash refunds and unapplied customer credit are deferred** to a follow-up ticket —
they require extending the aging subsidiary to carry credit balances (ADR-019).

**Files changed:**
- Schema: `src/db/schema/credit-memos.ts` (new); `audit.ts` (`CREDIT_MEMO_ISSUED/VOIDED`); `schema/index.ts`.
- Service: `src/server/credit-memos/{index,errors}.ts` (new); `src/validation/credit-memo.ts` (new).
- Shared derivation: `src/server/reports/open-balance.ts` — credit memos added as the third reduction source.
- Capability: `credit_memo.view` / `credit_memo.create` in `rbac/capabilities.ts` (+ `tests/unit/rbac.test.ts`).
- Tests: `tests/integration/credit-memos.test.ts` (new); **GL-T020** in `gl-regression.test.ts`; `ar-aging.test.ts` case; `isolation.test.ts` `credit_memos` descriptor.

**Migrations created:** `0019_credit_memos.sql` — creates the `credit_memo_status` enum, the
`CREDIT_MEMO_ISSUED/VOIDED` audit actions, and the `credit_memos` table (composite FKs to
invoice/customer/revenue-account, `amount > 0`, `UNIQUE(company_id,id)`, three indexes). Additive;
`CREDIT_MEMO` source type already existed. No trigger change (0018 already allows it).

**DB constraints added:** the three same-company composite FKs, the positivity CHECK, the standing
tenancy UNIQUE.

**Tests added:** `credit-memos.test.ts` (13 — posting Dr Sales Returns / Cr A/R customer-tagged,
partial/full → PAID, guards for non-REVENUE / over-balance / not-open / closed-period, tenancy,
void reverses + reopens); **GL-T020** (release gate — credit memo reconciles subsidiary⇔control,
and after void); an `ar-aging` bucket case; a `credit_memos` isolation descriptor.

**Test results:** lint ✓ · typecheck ✓ · unit ✓ (175) · integration ✓ (credit-memos 13, gl 20;
full suite as the gate) · build ✓. e2e n/a (no UI in this ticket).

**Accounting implications:** A/R can now be reduced for returns/allowances through a document the
subsidiary sees (`Dr Sales Returns / Cr A/R`, customer-tagged), reducing the invoice's open balance
so the reconciliation holds. Void is by reversal (invariant 3). No stored balance (invariant 2).

**Security implications:** company-scoped + authorized (`credit_memo.*`, fail closed); returns
account validated in-company/ACTIVE/REVENUE; A/R line customer-tagged via composite FK; posts only
through `postEntryCore` (invariant 8). Cross-company ids read as genuine misses; a `credit_memos`
isolation descriptor proves it.

**Decisions made not specified in the ticket:** ADR-019 — Model A scope (applied-to-invoice; defer
unapplied credit + refunds), caller-supplied REVENUE returns account, PAID on clear.

**Known limitations:** a fully credited invoice reads as PAID (a distinct terminal status is a later
nicety); no cash refunds / unapplied credit (deferred, ADR-019); no UI (reports/UI are later tickets).

**Recommended follow-up:** a "customer credits & refunds (unapplied credit + cash refunds)" ticket
that extends the aging subsidiary to carry credit balances; then LL-052/053 hardening and LL-054/055
(statements + reporting UI).
