# LL-033 — Immutability Enforcement and Reversal Engine


**Branch** `feat/ll-033-reversal-engine`

> Implement LL-033: posted-entry immutability at the service layer, and the reversal
> engine.
>
> ### Immutability
> There is no edit-posted-entry function, and none will be added. Attempts to modify a
> posted entry's transaction date, posting date, company, source, lines, accounts,
> debits, or credits return `POSTED_ENTRY_IMMUTABLE`. Draft entries remain editable
> subject to permissions.
>
> The LL-030 triggers already enforce this at the database. This ticket adds the service
> layer so callers receive a typed domain error rather than a raw constraint violation.
> **Test both layers** — service rejection, and raw SQL rejection with the service
> bypassed entirely.
>
> ### Reversal
>
> Reversal **never modifies the original entry's lines.** It creates a new entry:
> original debit becomes reversal credit, original credit becomes reversal debit, same
> accounts, same amounts, preserved descriptive and source linkage.
>
> - Set `reversal_of_id` on the new entry and `reversed_by_id` on the original.
> - The original's status may become `REVERSED` while all its financial records remain
>   exactly as posted. Historical visibility is preserved — the original is never
>   hidden, deleted, or altered.
> - The reversal date follows **ADR-007**. Implement what that ADR decided.
> - The reversal must land in an `OPEN` period. If the original's period is closed,
>   **do not reopen or mutate it** — post the reversal into an authorized open period
>   per the ADR.
> - Reversal is a `LedgerService` operation: same authorization, same period validation,
>   same balance validation, same atomicity, same audit event, all in one transaction.
> - Reversing an already-reversed entry fails with a typed error. Reversing a reversal
>   is permitted only if ADR-007 says so — implement what it decided, and if it is
>   silent, stop and ask rather than choosing.
>
> ### Tests
> Single-line-pair reversal; multi-line reversal; reversal when the original period is
> closed; reversal into a closed period rejected; double reversal attempt; cross-company
> reversal attempt; reversal by a user lacking the capability; concurrent reversal of
> the same entry producing exactly one reversal.
>
> **The decisive assertion:** for every reversal test, the net effect of the original
> plus the reversal across every account is exactly zero, computed with `decimal.js`
> from the journal lines. Not approximately zero.
