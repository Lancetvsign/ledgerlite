# LL-073 — Year-end closing entries

**Branch** `feat/ll-073-year-end-close` · **Status** Implemented (see §11) · **Origin** roadmap (the
LL-072 follow-up: make Retained Earnings real instead of derived).

> A year-end close posts one entry that zeroes a fiscal year's revenue/COGS/expense accounts into Retained
> Earnings, source-typed `CLOSING`, dated the fiscal-year end. Set-once per year (reuses the source-once
> index); reopen reverses it. The Income Statement excludes closing entries + their reversals; the Balance
> Sheet keeps counting them so equity total is invariant across a close. Closing does NOT lock periods
> (separate `/periods` action). Full design in ADR-031.

**Test results:** lint ✓ / typecheck ✓ / unit ✓ (182) / build ✓ (`npm run ci`); `db:check` clean. The
year-end suite is DB-backed and runs on the PR's CI shards, not locally against the shared dev branch.

**Accounting implications:** Retained Earnings becomes a real posted balance after a close (its first
consumer); closing zeroes the year's P&L into it. Balances stay derived from journal lines — the closing is
just posted lines. The Income Statement reports true operating activity regardless of close/reopen; the
Balance Sheet reflects actual ledger state as-of a date; equity total is invariant across a close.

**Security implications:** `period.close` (LEDGER_WRITERS) gates the page, the service, and reopen; company
from the session context. Idempotent on a per-render key; set-once enforced in the DB.

**Decisions made not specified in the ticket:** closing is separate from period status-close (product-owner
choice); it excludes closing entries AND their reversals from the Income Statement (needed for a correct
reopened-year P&L); reuses `period.close` (no rbac-matrix churn); posts directly to RE rather than via an
income-summary temporary account. Recorded as ADR-031.

**Known limitations:** closing does not lock periods (do that separately); no multi-year bulk close; no
income-summary intermediate account; the close UI's default period is a plain prior-calendar-year date
(the service normalises it).

**Recommended follow-up:** optionally couple period-lock into close; a cash-flow statement; comparative
columns; a formal income-summary model if ever preferred.
