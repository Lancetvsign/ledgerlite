# LL-074 — Cash-Flow Statement (indirect) + account cash-flow classification

**Branch** `feat/ll-074-cash-flow` · **Status** Implemented (see §11) · **Origin** roadmap (the third core
statement, after LL-072 balance sheet + income statement).

> The Cash-Flow Statement needs a classification the chart lacked, so this adds a nullable per-account
> `cashFlowCategory` (operating/investing/financing/cash), seeded on the default chart and editable, then
> derives an indirect 3-section statement reconciling beginning → ending cash. Full design in ADR-032.

**DB constraints added:** none.

**Tests added** — `tests/integration/cash-flow.test.ts`: the seeded chart carries categories; a period with
a cash sale, a credit sale (Δ operating asset), an expense on credit (Δ operating liability), an equipment
purchase (investing) and an owner contribution (financing) splits into the right sections and **reconciles**
(`netChangeInCash === endingCash − beginningCash`); an uncategorized balance-sheet account lands in its own
bucket and still reconciles; a **year-end close in the period leaves the statement unchanged** (closing
excluded, never touches cash); `from > to` rejected; non-member denied.

**Test results:** lint ✓ / typecheck ✓ / unit ✓ (182) / build ✓ (`npm run ci`); `db:check` clean. The
cash-flow suite is DB-backed and runs on the PR's CI shards.

**Accounting implications:** cash-flow is derived from journal lines (invariant 2), never stored. The
indirect identity `ΔCash = NetIncome + Σ adjustments` is enforced by the reconciliation check; income-
statement accounts roll into net income; closing entries/reversals are excluded so a close doesn't
double-count. LedgerLite now has all three core statements.

**Security implications:** `report.view` gates the report; `account.manage` gates classification. Company
from the session context. Reads only.

**Decisions made not specified in the ticket:** the per-account `cashFlowCategory` (product-owner choice,
ADR-032); a visible Uncategorized bucket so nothing is silently misfiled; cash identified by the `CASH`
category rather than a new system-account type; depreciation add-back handled implicitly by categorisation.

**Known limitations:** direct method not offered; custom accounts created before this default to
Uncategorized until classified; no separate depreciation/non-cash-addback modelling; no comparative
columns; classification quality depends on users categorising custom accounts.

**Recommended follow-up:** comparative (prior-period) columns across the three statements; a direct-method
option; optionally a `CASH` system-account type; the LL-072 fiscal-aware default period for period reports.
