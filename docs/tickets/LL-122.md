# LL-122 — Consolidated organization statements

**Branch** `feat/ll-122-consolidated-statements` · **Status** implemented · **Origin** owner's choice for LL-122 (2026-09-28): the Sprint 7 deferral "consolidated organization financial statements" · **Decision** ADR-047 (owner decided access, layout, matching and in-transit presentation) · **Migration** none

Two new reports under Reports: the **Consolidated Balance Sheet** and the **Consolidated Income Statement**. Each is a
worksheet: every company in your organization side by side, an Eliminations column and the consolidated total.
Intercompany balances ("Due from" / "Due to") are eliminated. Anything that doesn't net, such as a transfer still in
transit, appears on its own flagged line. The group balance sheet balances.
