# LL-071 — Opening balances (mid-life conversion entry)

**Branch** `feat/ll-071-opening-balances` · **Status** Implemented (see §11) · **Origin** roadmap
(post-Gate-5 feature; the `OPENING_BALANCE` source type + `Opening Balance Equity` account were reserved
by ADR-018/ADR-025 for exactly this).

> A company adopting LedgerLite mid-life seeds its non-control balance-sheet accounts as of a conversion
> date. One balanced entry posts through `postEntryCore` source-typed `OPENING_BALANCE`, with **Opening
> Balance Equity** as the auto-plug. **A/R and A/P are excluded** (they reconcile to a subsidiary — enter
> outstanding invoices/bills instead). Set-once; correct by void + re-enter. Full design in ADR-029.

**Test results:** lint ✓ / typecheck ✓ / unit ✓ (182) / build ✓ (`npm run ci`); `db:check` clean. The new
opening-balances suite is DB-backed (integration) and runs on the PR's CI shards, not locally against the
shared dev branch (per contract).

**Accounting implications:** opening balances are ordinary posted journal lines source-typed
`OPENING_BALANCE`; balances stay derived (invariant 2). OBE is the balancing clearing account (its first
use); the residual is reclassed to Retained Earnings by a normal journal entry (not automated). A/R/A/P are
kept out so aging⇔control reconciliation cannot be broken by a lump control-account balance.

**Security implications:** `journal.post` (LEDGER_WRITERS) gates both the page and the service (fail-closed,
company from the session, never a form field). Idempotency on a per-render key guards double-submit.

**Decisions made not specified in the ticket:** OBE auto-plug; set-once via a partial unique index + void;
reuse `journal.post` (avoid the exhaustive rbac-matrix churn a new capability forces); dedicated audit
actions rather than reusing `JOURNAL_ENTRY_POSTED`. Recorded as ADR-029.

**Known limitations:** opening A/R/A/P balances are out of scope (must be entered as invoices/bills); OBE→
Retained Earnings reclass is manual; opening balances are a single entry (no incremental build-up).

**Recommended follow-up:** a subsidiary-aware opening-A/R/A/P flow (opening invoices/bills whose offset is
OBE); optionally an OBE→Retained Earnings reclass helper.
