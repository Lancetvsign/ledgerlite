# LL-022 — Accounting Periods, Close and Reopen


**Branch** `feat/ll-022-accounting-periods`

> Implement LL-022: accounting periods and their lifecycle.
>
> Table `accounting_periods`: `id`, `company_id`, `start_date`, `end_date`, `status`
> (`OPEN` / `CLOSED`), `closed_at`, `closed_by`, `reopened_at`, `reopened_by`.
>
> Generate periods from the company's fiscal year configuration; monthly is acceptable
> for this implementation. Respect ADR-005 for dates and timezones — do not let
> server-local time determine period boundaries.
>
> **Prevent overlapping periods within a company at the database level.** Use an
> exclusion constraint over a date range with `company_id` equality
> (`EXCLUDE USING gist (company_id WITH =, daterange(start_date, end_date, '[]') WITH &&)`,
> enabling `btree_gist`). Application-level overlap checking races under concurrent
> generation; the constraint does not. Verify with two concurrent inserts.
>
> Standard tenancy constraints apply.
>
> **Services:**
> - `getAccountingPeriod(companyId, date)` — resolves per **ADR-002**. Read that ADR
>   and implement exactly what it decided about `transaction_date` vs `posting_date`.
> - `assertPeriodOpen(companyId, date)` — build it now even though no posting exists
>   yet. LL-031 will depend on it, and it must be the single place this rule lives.
>   Throw a typed `PERIOD_CLOSED` error.
>
> **Close and reopen** require the `period.close` capability via `requirePermission`.
> Record who and when for both. Both write audit events —
> `ACCOUNTING_PERIOD_CLOSED` and `ACCOUNTING_PERIOD_REOPENED` — with before and after
> state, in the same transaction as the status change.
>
> Period date ranges are not editable once financial activity exists. Financial activity
> arrives in Sprint 3, so structure the guard now and leave a clearly marked hook.
>
> Simple UI for viewing periods, with explicit confirmation before close or reopen.
>
> **Tests:** date lookup including both period boundaries; fiscal years not starting in
> January; overlap rejected by the database constraint; concurrent overlapping inserts;
> authorized close; unauthorized close denied; authorized reopen; wrong-company period;
> closing an already-closed period; reopening an already-open period; audit event
> written for both transitions; audit event absent when the transition is rolled back.
