# LL-125 — Consolidated cash-flow statement

**Branch** `feat/ll-125-consolidated-cash-flow` · **Status** implemented · **Origin** owner's choice for LL-125 (2026-09-29): LL-122's recommended follow-up · **Decision** ADR-047 amendment · **Migration** none

A third consolidated report: the **Consolidated Cash-Flow Statement**. Every company in the organization appears side
by side, with an Eliminations column and the consolidated total. Money moving between the companies cancels out
across the group. A transfer one company has recorded and the other hasn't yet appears as cash in transit. The
statement reconciles to the group's cash.
