# LL-043 — Customer payments: receive, apply to A/R, void

**Branch** `feat/ll-043-payments`

> Receive a customer payment, apply it to one or more of that customer's OPEN invoices, and
> post **Dr deposit / Cr Accounts Receivable** through `LedgerService`; a fully-paid invoice
> becomes PAID. Void reverses the entry and reverts the invoices. Sprint 4 (A/R). See **ADR-015**.

## What was built

- **`payments` + `payment_applications`** tables (composite-FK tenancy, positivity checks). A
  payment's `amount` is Σ(applications), service-derived (ADR-015) — a document total, not a
  balance. `payment_status` = POSTED/VOID.
- **`receivePayment`** — validates the customer, an ASSET deposit account, and each application
  (OPEN invoice, of this customer, ≤ its open balance), then in ONE transaction inserts the
  payment + applications, posts Dr deposit / Cr A/R (A/R customer-tagged, source `CUSTOMER_PAYMENT`,
  source-once via `postEntryCore`), and marks fully-paid invoices PAID.
- **`voidPayment`** — reverses the entry (`reverseEntryCore`), marks the payment VOID, and reverts
  any invoice it had paid back to OPEN. Atomic.
- **`voidInvoice` guard** — refuses an invoice with any live payment (`INVOICE_HAS_PAYMENTS`); void
  the payment first (prevents an untracked customer credit).
- **`resolveSystemAccount`** lifted from `invoices` to `@/server/accounts` (shared with payments).

**Accounting implications:** money in is now recorded and applied; every amount is derived and reversible; no account or per-invoice balance is stored (invariant 2). A/R and open balances derive from journal lines + applications.

**Security implications:** `receivePayment`/`voidPayment` authorize `payment.create` and fail closed; the ledger cores stay mechanical behind a capability check; cross-company ids read as a genuine miss; tenancy is structural (composite FKs on payments + applications, proven in isolation.test.ts).

**Decisions not specified in the ticket:** ADR-015 (multi-invoice fully-applied model; Dr deposit / Cr A/R; deposit must be an ASSET; PAID/void interplay + the `voidInvoice` guard). Lifted `resolveSystemAccount` into the accounts service (shared).

**Known limitations:** no overpayment / prepayment / on-account credit (fully-applied only); one deposit per payment (no Undeposited-Funds batch deposit); void handles POSTED payments only; payments have no sequential number (use `reference`).

**Recommended follow-up:** LL-044/045 invoice + payment UI; LL-046 A/R aging (builds on the applications); customer credits / refunds (CUSTOMER_REFUND) and the deposit workflow.
