# LL-126 — Cash-basis income statement

**Branch** `feat/ll-126-cash-basis` · **Status** implemented · **Origin** "start LL-126" (2026-09-29): the last item Sprint 7 deferred; the owner decided the rules (income statement only; credit memos, write-offs and vendor credits left out; other entries as posted) · **Decision** ADR-048 · **Migration** none

The income statement and the consolidated income statement now have an **Accrual | Cash** switch. On a cash basis:
- an invoice's revenue counts when the customer pays, and a bill's expense when it is paid;
- a payment is spread over the invoice or bill it pays, with sales tax left out;
- credit memos, write-offs and vendor credits are left out;
- everything else counts as posted, including bank postings and journal entries.
