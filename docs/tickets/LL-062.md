# LL-062 — Bill payments + A/P control-account lock

**Branch** `feat/ll-062-bill-payments`

**Status** Backlog · **Origin** Sprint 6 plan (Accounts Payable). See
[SPRINT-6-PLAN.md](../SPRINT-6-PLAN.md). Mirror of LL-043/045 (payments); extends ADR-018 (the 0018
control-account guard) to A/P.

> Paying a vendor settles the payable. The mirror of receiving a customer payment, with the cash
> flowing the other way.
>
> ### Schema
> A `bill_payments` (+ `bill_payment_applications`) pair mirroring payments/applications: `vendor_id`,
> `payment_date`, a `cash_account_id` (the account money leaves from), `amount` (= Σ applications),
> composite FKs to same-company vendor / bills / cash account, positivity CHECKs, and a
> `(bill_payment_id, bill_id)` unique. One migration.
>
> ### Posting (through `LedgerService`)
> **Dr A/P (`amount`, vendor-tagged) / Cr Cash**, applied to the vendor's OPEN bills (each locked
> `FOR UPDATE`, must belong to the vendor, ≤ its open balance), marking a bill **PAID** when its
> open balance reaches zero. The cash account must be in-company, ACTIVE, ASSET, and **not** the A/P
> control. `voidBillPayment` reverses and reverts PAID→OPEN under an explicit `FOR UPDATE`.
>
> ### A/P control-account lock (structural)
> **Generalize the LL-050 0018 trigger** so a manual `JOURNAL_ENTRY` line into the **A/P** control
> account is rejected too — one `BEFORE INSERT` guard covering every control account that has a
> subsidiary (A/R and A/P). Keeps A/P moved only by documents, so the aging⇔control tie is
> structural. Migration replaces the trigger function (mirror of 0018).
>
> ### Rules
> `bill_payment.view` (EVERYONE) / `bill_payment.create` (ALL_WRITERS) / `bill_payment.void`
> (LEDGER_WRITERS); update the rbac matrix. Over-application blocked with decimal.js vs the derived
> open balance.
>
> ### Tests
> Full/partial payment; PAID←→OPEN on void; over-apply / wrong-vendor / non-asset-cash rejected;
> **raw-SQL** manual JE into A/P **rejected** (structural), A/R still rejected, other system accounts
> still postable; **GL-Txxx** bill-payment reconciliation.
>
> ### Not in scope
> Vendor credits (LL-063); the aging/statement (LL-064); electronic payment / check printing.
