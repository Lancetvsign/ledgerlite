# LL-063 — Vendor credits (debit memos)

**Branch** `feat/ll-063-vendor-credits`

**Status** Backlog · **Origin** Sprint 6 plan (Accounts Payable). See
[SPRINT-6-PLAN.md](../SPRINT-6-PLAN.md). The structural mirror of LL-051 (customer credit memos).

> A vendor credit (debit memo) reduces what we owe a vendor without cash — a return or allowance
> against a bill. The A/P analogue of a credit memo.
>
> ### Schema
> A `vendor_credits` table mirroring `credit_memos`: `vendor_id`, `bill_id`, `credit_date`, `amount`,
> a contra `account_id` (the expense/returns account credited), `status` (`POSTED`/`VOID`),
> composite FKs, positivity CHECK. One migration.
>
> ### Posting (through `LedgerService`)
> **Dr A/P (vendor-tagged) / Cr Expense-contra**, source type reusing the A/P convention, reducing
> the bill's open balance exactly like a bill payment does. A credit that clears the bill marks it
> **PAID**; voiding reopens it. Applied per-bill (mirror ADR-019).
>
> ### Shared A/P open-balance
> Extend the **shared A/P open-balance derivation** (the mirror of `open-balance.ts`): a bill's open
> balance = `total − Σ(non-void bill-payments + vendor-credits)`, used by the aging (LL-064) and the
> over-application guards. Extend **`voidBill`'s adjustments guard** (mirror `INVOICE_HAS_ADJUSTMENTS`)
> to count vendor credits, so voiding a bill with a live credit is refused.
>
> ### Rules
> `vendor_credit.view` (EVERYONE) / `vendor_credit.create` (ALL_WRITERS) / `vendor_credit.void`
> (LEDGER_WRITERS); update the rbac matrix. Over-credit beyond open balance rejected (decimal.js).
>
> ### Tests
> Credit reduces the payable and reconciles (subsidiary⇔control moves together, **GL-Txxx**); clears
> → PAID; void restores; over-credit rejected; voiding a bill with a live credit refused. Company
> scoping / no leak.
>
> ### Not in scope
> Unapplied vendor credit (credit not tied to a bill); refunds from a vendor; the UI (LL-065).
